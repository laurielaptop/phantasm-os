documents use it, and what you may do with it.

identify itself. Please do not reuse, modify or

## What is used

| Asset | Files | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal lockup (emblem and wordmark) | `PH-LOGO-003-lockup-black.svg`, `PH-LOGO-003-lockup-yellow.svg` | README and document headers: black on light themes, yellow on dark |
| Wordmark | `PH-LOGO-001-wordmark-black.svg`, `PH-LOGO-001-wordmark-yellow.svg` | Available for narrow spaces |
| Full emblem | `PH-LOGO-002-emblem-colour.svg` (yellow roundel), `PH-LOGO-002-emblem-black.svg`, `PH-LOGO-002-emblem-yellow.svg` | Available |
| Micro emblem (one arc) | `PH-LOGO-002-emblem-micro-colour.svg` | For sizes below 32 px |

All are vector, with no fonts or external resources. The slogan is
**Embrace the glitch!**, always with that capitalisation and punctuation.

## Palette

| Colour | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Yellow | `#FFD42A` | Logo on dark backgrounds, the roundel |
| Ink | `#121212` | Logo on light backgrounds |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Reversed logo on dark backgrounds |

Use ink or outlined yellow on light backgrounds, and white or yellow on dark
ones. Do not put unoutlined yellow on white.

## Sizes and clear space

| Asset | Minimum width | Clear space around the visible artwork |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | 180 px | One letter-stem width |
| Full emblem | 32 px | The radius of its central disc |
| Micro emblem | 16 px | The radius of its central disc |
| Lockup | 320 px | The radius of the emblem's central disc |

Scale clear space with the artwork. Keep the aspect ratio, do not stretch, add
effects to or redraw the logos, and use the emblem alone below the lockup
minimum. These are screen rules; no print specification has been set.
