# Security policy

Phantasm runs on a vintage instrument and in your browser, and handles no
accounts or personal data. If you find a problem with the editor that could
affect your computer or your files, please report it privately through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/laurielaptop/phantasm-os/security/advisories/new)
rather than a public issue. Everything else belongs in the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/laurielaptop/phantasm-os/issues/new/choose).

Verify downloads against the `SHA256SUMS` file attached to each release.
